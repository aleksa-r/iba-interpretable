# ABC over the whole space of formulas: different starting positions, whole-vector moves, zero allowed

## 0. Why

The first study (SPEC-2026-11-13) found that the ABC refinement of the prior formulas (A) never
beat the fixed prior (E1), that its winner passed the acceptance test in 16–28 % of the blocks,
and that only the colony ensemble (B) helped, by under 1 % of the loss. That ABC is tied to the
prior: every food source starts at the prior or one move from it, a neighbour is one move in one
group, no solution may be more than 4 status changes away, and scouts restart next to the prior.

Found while preparing this spec (synthetic data, section 4): the first study's acceptance test
was not clean. The five selection folds have overlapping validation windows (78 weeks, step
33–34; 18 months, step 9–10), so 44–45 of the 78 weekly rows of the "outer" fold (8–9 of 18 monthly) are
also validation rows of the last fitness fold: the search had already used them. On synthetic
returns without any signal the winner "gains" +0.5 % to +2.8 % on that fold and passes the test
in 23–27 % of the blocks; on the outer rows the search has not used it LOSES 5 % (A) to 22 %
(graded search) and passes in 8–20 %. Recorded in `docs/DEVIATIONS.md`. This spec uses the clean
rows and re-tests the first study's ABC with them.

Owner, 2026-11-14: "find better way to implement ABC. Start searching from different position.
Maybe search full vector at once and allow min = 0 or something". This spec turns the three ideas
into four searches, each adding one thing to the previous one, and tests them on the development
series under the first study's protocol. The owner's wording leaves the vector open; the reading
used here is stated in section 3 and flagged in the report.

## 1. Questions and expectations (written before any result)

Q1 Different starting positions: does an ABC whose colony is spread over the whole space (and is
   not tied to the prior) find formulas that are better OUT OF SAMPLE than the prior?
Q2 Whole vector at once: does moving many coordinates of one formula vector in a single step
   help, on crisp formulas (V) or on graded ones (G)?
Q3 Zero allowed / zero start: does a search that may switch any factor, group or the whole
   formula off, or that starts from nothing (Z), end with sparser and better models?
Q4 Does the colony of a whole-space search make a better ensemble than B?

Expected, from the first study (search comparison: 10–12 % inner gain shrank to under 1 % on the
outer fold for four algorithms): more freedom gives a larger inner gain, a larger loss of it out
of sample and a lower acceptance rate; winners without the acceptance test are worse than E1;
with the test they mostly fall back to the prior and equal E1. Open: the ensembles (a spread
colony is more diverse, its members individually weaker) and volatility, where there is signal.
Returns and tails: no method is expected to beat the reference. A null result is reported as such.

## 2. Data and cells

Development series only (48; `data/split_manifest.json`); panels, targets, labels, horizons and
the walk-forward protocol exactly as SPEC-2026-11-13 sections 2, 3 and 7. Cells: every return and
volatility cell and, of the tail cells, those at q = 10 % — 458 of the 842 cells, 18 tasks
(`ibanext.protocol.study.abc2_tasks`). Reason for the tail subset: tails are 68 % of the cells and
the first study found no IBA skill there at any q; q = 10 % is the middle size and the one of the
search comparison. The forecasts of E1, A, B, group PCA and the references for these cells are
the first study's (artifacts on disk, same dates and labels; the report checks that).

## 3. The searches (`ibanext.search.vector`, wired in `ibanext.search.run`)

Fixed for all four, as for A: the fitness (mean loss on fitness folds 1–4 of the linear estimator
at ridge α = 1 / logistic C = 1 + 0.5 % of the prior's fitness per literal in excess of the
prior), budget 2 000 fitness evaluations of distinct candidates per block, colony 20, abandonment
after 10 failures, rank-proportional onlookers, the winner = best fitness, and ONE acceptance test
of the winner against the prior (kept only if better by more than one block standard error;
otherwise the prior). At most 3 literals per group for crisp formulas, as in A. The prior is
still the generic r8 formula set.

**The acceptance test uses clean rows only** (`FitnessEvaluator.clean`): the rows j of the outer
fold with j ≥ (last validation row of any fitness fold) + h, so that no label of the test overlaps
a label the search has seen. Weekly: about 34 / 31 / 21 rows for h = 1 / 4 / 13; monthly: about
10 / 8 / 5 / 0 for h = 1 / 3 / 6 / 12. With fewer than 2 clean rows (monthly h = 12) the prior is kept, so the
tested winner equals E1 there by construction. The winner without the test and the ensemble do
not depend on this.

- **W — different starting positions.** A's discrete moves without the leash: no distance bound.
  Colony = the prior + 19 random formulas of the whole space (each group on with probability 0.5,
  then one or two predicates with random polarity). A scout restarts at a random formula.
- **V — whole vector, crisp formulas.** A food source is one vector x ∈ [−1, 1]^D, one coordinate
  per predicate of every group (D = 67 weekly). Decoding: predicate j is in its group's
  conjunction iff |x_j| ≥ 0.5, asserted if x_j > 0, negated otherwise; more than 3 in a group →
  the 3 largest |x_j|; none → the group is off; every group off → the EMPTY formula, which is
  allowed (the model is then its intercept: the historical mean, HAR or the base rate). A move:
  v_j = x_ij + φ_j (x_ij − x_kj) + ψ_j (g_j − x_ij), φ ~ U(−1, 1), ψ ~ U(0, 1.5), k another source,
  g the best vector so far (gbest-guided ABC), clipped to the box, applied to every coordinate
  with probability MR (at least one). Start and scouts as W, formulas encoded with their literals
  in ±[0.5, 1] and the other coordinates inside (−0.5, 0.5).
- **G — whole vector, graded formulas, zero allowed.** The vector is used as it is: |x_j| is the
  DEGREE u_j to which literal j is in its group's conjunction (|x_j| < 0.1 counts as exactly 0,
  not used), the sign is the polarity, and the group's feature is
  Π_j [(1 − u_j) + u_j · lit_j]. Expanded, this is a convex combination of conjunctions, i.e. a
  logical aggregation of generalized Boolean polynomials under the product; at u ∈ {0, 1} it is
  the crisp conjunction (tested to 1e-14). The fitness is continuous in the vector; the literal
  term uses the total inclusion Σ u_j. Colony = the prior (±1 on its literals, 0 elsewhere) + 19
  vectors uniform in the box; scouts uniform in the box; moves as V.
- **Z — G started from zero.** Every coordinate of every source starts inside (−0.1, 0.1): the
  whole colony is the empty formula, and inclusion grows out of it. Scouts restart at zero. The
  prior is evaluated (penalty scale, acceptance reference) but is not a food source.

Reading of "full vector, min = 0": one real vector over all predicates of all groups whose lower
bound of use is zero. G and Z take it literally (ABC's native real-vector form; requires graded
inclusion); V keeps crisp, readable formulas. Not run: a vector over the 2^n atoms of each group
(general Boolean functions; over 1 000 dimensions here).

Configurations per search s ∈ {w, v, g, z} and seed (3 seeds, linear estimator only):
`iba_<s>|lin` the winner if it passed the acceptance test, else the prior; `iba_<s>r|lin` the
winner without the test (diagnostic); `iba_<s>b` the final colony as an ensemble (as B). From the
first study's ABC (the same search, re-run): `iba_ar|lin`, its winner without the test, and
`iba_ac|lin`, its winner with the clean-rows test. 14 configurations, 12 of them new searches.

## 4. Settings chosen on synthetic data

`scripts/calibrate_abc2_synthetic.py`: a weekly panel of the real shape (956 weeks, 8 groups × 4
factors, two windows, D = 67) with persistent factors; returns with no signal and with a weak
planted signal that the prior only partly describes; return h = 4 and large loss q = 10 %; 3 data
seeds × 2 blocks × 2 search seeds per setting; budget 2 000. Only the modification rate MR of the
vector moves was chosen here, by a rule set before the output was read: the highest mean inner
gain on the panels with signal; within 5 % of the best, the larger MR.

| setting | inner gain, signal | inner gain, no signal |
|---|---|---|
| A (first study) | 0.160 | 0.158 |
| W | 0.235 | 0.229 |
| V, MR 0.05 / 0.1 / 0.3 / 1.0 | 0.300 / 0.308 / 0.321 / 0.313 | 0.296 / 0.296 / 0.319 / 0.317 |
| G, MR 0.1 / 0.3 / 1.0 | 0.351 / 0.388 / 0.451 | 0.348 / 0.364 / 0.445 |
| Z, MR 0.1 / 0.3 / 1.0 | 0.333 / 0.351 / 0.399 | 0.313 / 0.332 / 0.392 |

Chosen: **MR = 1.0 for V, G and Z** — the whole vector moves at once. The inner gain is the same
with and without signal: on these panels the searches fit noise equally well, and more freedom
fits more of it (the first study's 10–12 % on real data becomes 16 % here for A and 45 % for G).
Other settings were not searched: decoding threshold 0.5, "not used" below 0.1, ψ ≤ 1.5, random
formulas with each group on with probability 0.5.

Acceptance test on synthetic returns WITHOUT signal (`scripts/check_acceptance_synthetic.py`, 32
panels × 4 blocks, h = 4), winner before the test: gain on the whole outer fold +0.5 % (A), +2.8 % (W), +2.0 % (V), +1.4 % (G), +2.2 % (Z);
on the clean rows −4.8 %, −7.0 %, −13.2 %, −22.2 %, −19.2 %; passed with the whole fold
24 / 26 / 26 / 27 / 23 %, with the clean rows 20 / 16 / 9 / 8 / 10 %.

## 5. What is fixed and what is not claimed

Same engine fingerprint as the first study (the existing methods are unchanged; the fingerprint
cell reproduces). Three seeds. No tuning of any setting on development results: section 4 used
synthetic data only. This spec does not touch the confirmation series and makes no claim beyond
development data; whatever it finds, the guard is the confirmation run.

## 6. Statistics, reporting and the decision rule (`scripts/make_abc2_report.py`, written before the run)

1. Search diagnostics per search and target type, units = (cell, block), seeds averaged: inner
   gain, gain on the whole outer fold and gain on the clean rows over the prior (winner before
   the test), acceptance rate (clean rows; for A also under the first study's test), literals and
   groups of the winner, share of empty winners, distinct members and mean size of the colony.
2. Per (frequency, task), all development series of that frequency pooled on the same dates:
   paired date-block-bootstrap 95 % intervals (2 000 draws, block ≥ h) of the relative difference
   in the primary loss for: each `iba_<s>|lin`, `iba_<s>r|lin` and `iba_<s>b` against E1; each
   `iba_<s>b` against B and against group PCA; all against the type's reference; A (first study's
   test), `iba_ac`, `iba_ar` and B against E1 on the same cells for comparison.
3. SPA per task: does any of the 12 new configurations beat E1, B, group PCA, the reference.
4. Units (task × asset class) won and lost; spread across seeds.
5. Decision rule, applied mechanically. A candidate (`iba_<s>|lin` and `iba_ac|lin` against E1;
   `iba_<s>b` against B) QUALIFIES as the ABC step of the next spec if it is significantly better than its incumbent
   in more tasks than it is significantly worse AND its mean loss is lower in at least 3 of the 4
   target types. Among several, the lowest mean relative loss with the types weighted equally.
   If none qualifies, the first study's implementation stays and the result is reported as a null.
